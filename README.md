# Healthcare Claims Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing healthcare insurance claims data — covering denial rates, provider specialty trends, claim demographics, and location-based patterns to support claims operations decision-making.

**Tools Used:** Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query

**Author:** Priyadharshini Kanagaraj | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/priyadharshini-kanagaraj-) | [GitHub](https://github.com/priyadharshinikanagaraj11)

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains 5 pages:

1. **Overview** — High-level KPIs (Total Claims, Claim Amount, Denial/Approval/Pending Rate) and monthly claims trend
2. **Denial Analysis** — Denial trends by reason, specialty, and claim type
3. **Demographics** — Claims breakdown by patient age, gender
4. **Provider View** — Claim and denial patterns by medical specialty
5. **Executive Summary** — Summary view for leadership-level reporting

---

## Key Insights

- **General Practice has the highest denial rate at 35.45%**, indicating potential documentation or coding issues specific to this specialty.
- **Emergency claims show a higher denial rate than Inpatient claims**, despite the urgent nature of care — suggesting a review of emergency claim documentation standards may be needed.
- **Overall denial rate stands at 33.60%**, meaning roughly 1 in 3 claims is getting denied.
- **Approved, Denied, and Pending claims are almost equally distributed (~33% each)**, with the high Pending rate (32.58%) pointing to possible bottlenecks in the claims review workflow.
- **Paper submission claims show higher pending rates than Electronic submissions**, recommending a shift toward electronic claims processing to improve turnaround time.
